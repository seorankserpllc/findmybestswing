2. Unknown personal distance input: separate carry and rollout and retain normal strike variation.
3. Unsure whether sand or gap comes first: identify the missing bunker shot versus missing approach carry.
4. Between loft options: compare neighboring lofts and personal carries; uneven numbers alone do not justify replacement.
5. Limited budget: keep/borrow/used/stock/custom choices with full landed and return costs.
6. Premium-versus-value confusion: require demonstrated usefulness or a needed specification.
7. Sole mismatch: representative turf and sand trials, including the opposite condition.
8. Conflicting flex labels: reconcile the exact manufacturer part number and disclose Wedge versus Stiff.
9. Hand, weight, length and lie compatibility: skip this stock right-handed build when those needs differ.
10. Online order ambiguity: model/generation/part number, shaft, dimensions, grip, one-club quantity and inclusions checklist.
11. Return/used/retrofit risk: inspect condition and alterations; obtain written terms and qualified bending advice.
12. No repeatable decision: alternating comparison, normal misses retained, another day/lie, and keep-current tie rule.
