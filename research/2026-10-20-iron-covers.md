## Intent, gap and obstacles

Reader intent: decide whether individual covers solve a worthwhile problem, then choose a set that fits the clubs and normal routine. Segments: limited-budget beginner; transport-only user; walker/rider annoyed by clatter; large-head or left-handed buyer; golfer with several loft-marked wedges; wet-weather player; buyer with hand-comfort constraints; gift/customization shopper.

The gap addressed is the combination of a no-purchase verdict, transport-only alternative, exact ten-cover label mapping, full-bag fit/handling trial and primary care guidance. Product card follows the verdict and contains specific benefit, trade-off and skip advice. No claim that no competitor discusses moisture or fit.

Thirteen material obstacles resolved:

1. Unclear purpose: distinguish noise, cosmetic contact and transport.
2. Assumed necessity for beginners or expensive irons: explicit no-purchase option.
3. On-course inconvenience: transport-only alternative.
4. Whole-bag versus individual-head protection: no crush/airline guarantee.
5. Pack count versus useful coverage: map every actual club.
6. Conflicting AW/GW copy: reconcile with selected manufacturer and retailer photographs.
7. Large-head/short-iron/wedge differences: test every intended head.
8. Left-hand orientation: physical and label check; no universal fit claim.
9. Dexterity and glove use: repeat the real removal motion.
10. Wet storage and product-care instructions: maker-specific guidance and separate drying.
11. Premium/custom value: no invented scoring, durability or resale return.
12. Replacement and return cost: confirm single-cover supply and trial eligibility.
13. Retention and loss: pocket routine, normal bag movement and a clear keep/return decision.

Nine FAQs: beginner value, transport-only use, rust/care, oversized/left-handed fit, magnetic versus slip-on/zip, pack count, AW versus GW, resale/scoring expectations and losing covers/handling. Six situation rows, eight checklist items and a four-step proposed buyer trial. No trial is represented as performed by this team.
