Twelve obstacles resolved:

1. Driver clubhead speed confused with ball or iron speed: clarify the input.
2. Unknown speed: comparison remains possible; request proper monitor identification rather than derive speed from one drive.
3. Cross-brand compression numbers: explain methods and omit unverified figures.
4. Being between chart bands: no automatic replacement; test outcomes.
5. Premium minimum-speed myth: primary Titleist fitting explanation, no eligibility cutoff.
6. Soft sensation confused with stopping: identify cover facts and observe landing/release.
7. Ongoing budget/lost balls: delivered unit cost times personal replacement rate.
8. Longest shot overriding useful results: separate carry, total distance and ordinary misses.
9. Unclear evidence or tied result: repeat; keep current or cheaper ball, with no promised gains.
10. Used/bulk model uncertainty: initial consistent, identifiable small batch.
11. Exact online variant and weak pack labels: match generation, color, model code, count and condition; explicit yellow Pro V1 instead of a white image.
12. Return and future stock risk: check opened-box terms and retest before changing generations or buying bulk.
