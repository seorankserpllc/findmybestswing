## Fan-out and obstacles

Live People Also Ask: which irons a 20 handicap should play; best used irons at 20 handicap; how far a 20 handicap should hit a 7-iron; whether 20 handicap is “good.” Related searches included 15–20 handicap, mid-handicap seniors, 15 handicap and 10 handicap. Exclude the status/“good golfer” question because it does not change this purchase.

Nine useful FAQs added: iron category, used buying, 7-iron distance, blades/growing into clubs, senior/material/flex, 5-versus-6-iron start, AW compatibility, fitting before consistency, and retrofit versus replacement.

Resolved obstacles (12):
1. Handicap confused with iron need — approach-shot log and explicit keep path.
2. Unknown personal inputs — record strike, carry, direction and club-specific problem.
3. Between head categories — same standard for ordinary misses and useful flight.
4. Flex/material labels treated as prescriptions — exact build comparison.
5. Limited budget — whole fitted cost and keep/repair/used options.
6. Premium improvement assumed — no automatic generation benefit.
7. Long-iron redundancy — longest-club test and shorter-set alternative.
8. Wedge incompatibility — actual lofts and retained-club test.
9. Standard build may not fit — length/lie/shaft written fitting sheet.
10. Used or online identity uncertainty — photographs, alterations and order checklist.
11. No repeatable purchase test — alternating blocks, retained mishits, repeat after rest/turf.
12. Returns/warranty/retrofit risk — obtain applicable terms and builder quote before order.
