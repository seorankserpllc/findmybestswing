# Guide quality and SEO audit — October 21, 2026

## Scope and conclusion

Reviewed the structure, titles, verdicts, recommendations, links and shared rendering of all 13 guides. Re-researched and materially expanded the 20-handicap iron article. This is not a fresh manufacturer/retailer fact-check or five-result competitive review of every archived guide. Existing listing dates on other articles remain unchanged.

The archive has useful decision tables, checklists, FAQs, visible sources, clean paths, canonicals and a sitemap. Its weaknesses were an iron-roundup promise with only one model, no contextual guide-to-guide links, an empty initial app shell and little original visual utility. Passing a checklist did not establish competitive superiority. The common source-based authorship also does not substitute for documented firsthand experience.

Implemented: three differentiated iron models near the verdict; an accurate comparison title instead of an unsupported market-wide “best” claim; 27 curated contextual links across the 13 guides; a carry-gap worksheet in the iron and beginner-wedge guides; initial HTML with article text, links, metadata and matching JSON-LD for all 13 guides and their hub. Added build checks for missing sections, targets and contextual incoming links. Removed unsupported “tested” wording from the shared header/footer. Product suggestions remain near the buying verdict, with fit, limitations and the verified action together.

## What would make a credible attempt at first place

Use [Google's review guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews) as evidence requirements: original experience where claimed, fair alternatives, meaningful benefits/drawbacks and support for “best” judgments. Commission and document real tests or fittings, publish the actual method, equipment/builds, conditions, photos and limitations, and identify the real contributor with consent. Do not invent a credential or turn manufacturer copy into a test result.

Use [contextual crawlable links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable) and [complete rendered content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics) to make those answers discoverable. Relevant links have no magic target count. Initial HTML improves access for users and crawlers; it does not guarantee indexing or rank. FAQ markup is not a promise of a rich result.

Build a coherent fitting/maintenance topic library, earn relevant references through genuinely useful work, and maintain current evidence. Track search and buying outcomes. Ranking first depends on competing pages and Google's systems; no template, word count, publishing cadence or visual can guarantee it.

Remaining technical/trust backlog: the main client bundle still triggers Vite's 500 kB warning; homepage, catalog and product routes are outside this guide prerender change. Legacy product pages still need an evidence audit of “Tested” dates, editorial scores and performance assertions (for example the old Supersoft summary). Do not present the whole site as freshly fact-checked because the guide rendering passes.
