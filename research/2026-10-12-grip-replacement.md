## Twelve material obstacles resolved

1. Calendar versus actual condition: attributed maintenance interval plus inspection decision.
2. Dirt versus wear: clean/dry/reassess intact grips.
3. Surface slipping versus grip rotating on shaft: separate installation inspection.
4. One worn club versus full set: count affected clubs, separate putter.
5. Uncertain size or texture: trial one installed grip before bulk purchase.
6. Core/shaft/tape compatibility: builder check and recorded finished build.
7. Beginner/senior labels versus individual comfort: trial, no automatic size rule.
8. Premium price versus outcome: no assumed durability or score benefit.
9. Hidden total cost: labor/supplies/shipping/tax, avoid double-counting bundled labor.
10. Pack count versus kit contents: exact three-grip option and separately budgeted supplies.
11. Tee-time deadline versus installation drying: manufacturer minimum scoped to actual process.
12. Future reorder and return risk: written specification, seller terms before installation, dated maintenance notes.
